近年来，越来越多的城市开始重新思考公共空间的意义。过去，城市建设往往更关注交通效率和商业价值，而今天，人们逐渐意识到，一座宜居的城市还需要为步行、休息、交流和文化活动留下足够的空间。公共空间的改善看似只是增加了一些公园、座椅和步道，实际上却可能改变居民与城市相处的方式。一个人是否愿意走出家门，是否能够在日常生活中与陌生人自然地相遇，以及是否对自己居住的社区产生归属感，都与城市空间的设计密切相关。因此，如何在有限的土地资源中平衡效率与生活质量，已经成为许多城市必须面对的问题。
In recent years, more and more cities have begun to rethink the role of public space. In the past, urban development often placed greater emphasis on transport efficiency and commercial value. Today, however, people increasingly recognize that a livable city also needs to provide sufficient space for walking, resting, social interaction and cultural activities.
Improvements to public space may appear to involve little more than adding parks, benches and walkways, but they can in fact change the way residents relate to their city. Whether people are willing to leave their homes, whether they can naturally encounter strangers in their daily lives, and whether they develop a sense of belonging to the communities in which they live are all closely related to the design of urban space. Therefore, how to balance efficiency and quality of life within limited land resources has become a challenge that many cities must address.
