翻译练习 9月15日

今天材料取自 2026 年 9 月 8 日国务院关于城市更新的公开报道所涉及的内容。近期政策讨论提出，城市更新需要改善居住环境和民生，同时盘活公共空间、服务设施和生态资源，并兼顾历史文化保护。

近年来，随着人口结构、经济发展动力和房地产市场发生深刻变化，城市更新已经成为改善人居环境、增进民生福祉的重要途径。城市更新并不只是拆除旧建筑、建设新设施，也不能简单理解为扩大城市规模。真正高质量的城市更新，需要把人的需求放在更加重要的位置，通过盘活已有的公共空间、服务设施和生态资源，提高城市空间的使用效率和生活品质。

In recent years, with the deep changing taking place in population structure, economic growth power and housing market, the renewal process of cities has become an important factor on improving living environment and wellbeing for citizens. The renewal for urban cities does not only mean to destruct old buildings and build new ones, nor equal to simply expand the existing urban area. The high quality urban renewal - in the end - should put the needs of normal citizens as a more vital part, while improve the efficiency of urban area facilities and living quality to use existing public area, serving facilites and ecosystem resources in new ways.

在这一过程中，老旧住宅、社区、街区和厂区的改造需要统筹推进，城市基础设施的安全性和韧性也需要进一步提高。与此同时，不同城市有各自的历史、文化和空间特点。如果更新过程中只追求统一的建设标准，而忽视城市原有的风貌和历史遗产，一些不可替代的地方特色可能因此消失。因此，城市更新既要解决现实生活中的具体问题，也要考虑一座城市在长期发展中希望保留什么、改变什么以及创造什么。

During the process, old buildings, communities, blocks and factories should being considered together as a whole part to improve, while the basic facilities in urban area should be improved further on their security and stability. At the same time, different cities has different history, culture and spacial structures. If only a very same constructing standard taken into consideration, ignoring the original appearance and historical heritage, some places that shouldn't be replaced would have disappeared. Thus, the renewal of urban area should not only solve specific problems in daily life, but consider the things to keep, to change and to create during the long term progress of development.
